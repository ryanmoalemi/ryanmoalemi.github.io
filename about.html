About

Hi, I’m Ryan.

I'm unusually curious about things that have almost nothing to do with each other. I like spotting opportunities, questioning assumptions, and figuring out what really matters.

I think fast, sometimes faster than the evidence warrants. I've learned that a good theory isn't necessarily a fact. I enjoy taking complicated problems apart, finding the overlooked details, and turning ideas into something useful. I'm not particularly comfortable leaving things in limbo.
